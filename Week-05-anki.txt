Which of <code>IOException</code>, <code>IllegalArgumentException</code>, and <code>StackOverflowError</code> is checked?	<code>IOException</code> is checked. It must be caught or declared.<br><code>IllegalArgumentException</code> is a <code>RuntimeException</code>, so it is unchecked. <code>StackOverflowError</code> is an <code>Error</code>, a separate branch.	week5 exceptions
A catch of <code>Exception</code> comes before a catch of <code>RuntimeException</code>. Does that compile?	No. The specific type comes first.<br>Once <code>Exception</code> is caught, the <code>RuntimeException</code> catch is unreachable.	week5 exceptions
Does <code>catch (FileNotFoundException | IOException e)</code> compile?	No. Multi-catch alternatives cannot be related by inheritance.<br>Catch <code>FileNotFoundException</code> in its own clause before <code>IOException</code>. The multi-catch parameter is effectively final.	week5 exceptions
The catch throws <code>RuntimeException(&quot;catch&quot;)</code> and <code>finally</code> throws <code>RuntimeException(&quot;finally&quot;)</code>. What does the caller see?	The message <code>finally</code>. <code>getSuppressed()</code> is empty.<br>A <code>finally</code> that throws replaces the pending exception. It does not add that pending exception as a suppressed one.	week5 exceptions
A method sets <code>s</code> to <code>go try</code>, returns <code>s</code>, and <code>finally</code> appends <code> finally</code> and prints <code>s</code>. What is printed, and what is returned?	The print is <code>go try finally</code>.<br><code>finally</code> runs before the return completes. The returned string was already captured as <code>go try</code>.	week5 exceptions
Resources <code>A</code> then <code>B</code> are created in one try-with-resources. The body prints <code>-</code>. Close prints the lowercase name. What is the line?	<code>AB-ba</code>.<br>Close runs after the body, last created first.	week5 exceptions
The try-with-resources body throws <code>body</code>, and <code>close</code> throws <code>close</code>. Which exception propagates, and where is the other?	The message is <code>body</code>. <code>close</code> is suppressed on it.<br>This is the opposite of a <code>finally</code> block that throws.	week5 exceptions
Can you assign a try-with-resources variable inside the body?	No. The resource is effectively final, including a variable declared outside and named in the parentheses.	week5 exceptions
<code>BadAmount</code> extends <code>Exception</code>. <code>main</code> calls a method that throws it and does not catch or declare it. Does <code>main</code> compile?	No. A subclass of <code>Exception</code> that is not a <code>RuntimeException</code> is checked.<br>Extend <code>RuntimeException</code> only when you want an unchecked exception.	week5 dates
<code>LocalDate d = LocalDate.of(2026, 12, 21); d.plusDays(1);</code> What is <code>d</code>?	<code>2026-12-21</code>.<br><code>plusDays</code> returns a new date. Assign the result if you want to keep it.	week5 dates
What do <code>Period.between</code> of 21 Dec and 23 Dec, and <code>Duration.ofHours(2).plusMinutes(30)</code>, print?	<code>P2D</code> and <code>PT2H30M</code>.<br><code>Period</code> is date-based and uses <code>P</code>. <code>Duration</code> is time-based and uses <code>PT</code>.	week5 dates
What happens on <code>LocalDate.of(2026, 12, 21).plus(Duration.ofDays(1))</code>? Which of <code>LocalTime</code> and <code>Instant</code> accepts which amount?	It throws <code>UnsupportedTemporalTypeException</code>. A date has no time fields.<br><code>LocalTime</code> accepts a <code>Duration</code>. <code>LocalDate</code> accepts a <code>Period</code>. An <code>Instant</code> accepts a <code>Duration</code> and rejects a <code>Period</code>.	week5 dates
In <code>America/New_York</code>, what does <code>ZonedDateTime.of</code> do with local time 02:30 on 8 March 2026?	That local time does not exist. The result is <code>2026-03-08T03:30-04:00</code>.<br>The missing hour is moved forward across the spring-forward gap.	week5 dates
Local time 01:30 on 1 November 2026 happens twice in New York. Which offset does <code>ZonedDateTime.of</code> pick, and which call picks <code>-05:00</code>?	<code>of</code> picks the earlier offset, <code>-04:00</code>.<br><code>ZonedDateTime.ofLocal</code> with preferred offset <code>-05:00</code> picks the later, standard-time occurrence.	week5 dates
For 21 Dec 2026 at 15:04:05 in <code>Locale.US</code>, what are <code>yyyy-MM-dd</code>, <code>h:mm a</code>, <code>EEE</code>, and <code>D</code>?	<code>2026-12-21</code>, <code>3:04 PM</code>, <code>Mon</code>, and <code>355</code>.<br><code>HH</code> is the hour 0–23, so <code>HH:mm:ss</code> is <code>15:04:05</code>. <code>D</code> is the day of the year. A quoted <code>T</code> in <code>yyyy-MM-dd'T'HH:mm</code> stays a literal: <code>2026-12-21T15:04</code>.	week5 dates
For 21 Dec 2026, what do patterns <code>MMM d, yyyy</code>, <code>MMMM</code>, <code>q</code>, and <code>e</code> print in <code>Locale.US</code>?	<code>Dec 21, 2026</code>, <code>December</code>, <code>4</code>, and <code>2</code>.<br><code>MMM</code> is the short month. <code>MMMM</code> is the full month. <code>q</code> is the quarter. <code>e</code> is the numeric weekday, and Monday is 2 because the US week starts on Sunday.	week5 dates
What does <code>DateTimeFormatter.ISO_LOCAL_DATE</code> print for 21 Dec 2026, and what does pattern <code>ss</code> print for 15:04:05?	<code>2026-12-21</code> and <code>05</code>.<br><code>MM</code> is the month with a leading zero. A single <code>M</code> would print the month without one. <code>dd/MM/yyyy</code> for this date is <code>21/12/2026</code>.	week5 dates
